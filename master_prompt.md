```text
Act as a premium UI/UX designer and elite front-end developer. Build a professional, premium, and high-converting website for a physical/offline Korean Language Institute. 

CRITICAL STRATEGY RULES:
1. The institute is 100% physical/offline. It does NOT offer any online courses, digital portals, Zoom webinars, recorded video lessons, or student dashboards.
2. The website must clearly communicate: "Offline Classes Only — Visit the Institute for Learning." Never let it feel like an online learning platform.
3. Main Business Goal: Get students to contact via WhatsApp and visit the institute physically for admissions and face-to-face counseling. No forms or online booking systems.
4. Target Audience: Students preparing for EPS-TOPIK, general Korean language learners, prospective workers planning to emigrate to South Korea, and their parents seeking a highly disciplined coaching center.

### VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM & BRANDING

Aesthetic: Modern, premium educational, clean layout, traditional Korean-inspired subtle visual themes, micro-animations, clean spacing, soft shadows, rounded corners, sticky navigation.

Color Palette (HSL/HEX tailorable):
- Primary: Deep Korean Ink Charcoal (#1E2022) & Slate Navy (#0F172A) for strong academic authority.
- Secondary Accent: Traditional Crimson / Rose-Gold (#C23616 / #E15F41) representing Korean heritage.
- Highlights: WhatsApp green (#20BF6B) for high-converting CTAs.
- Backgrounds: Pristine white (#FFFFFF) paired with soft warm ivory/porcelain (#FAF9F6) for a high-end editorial feel.

Typography:
- Headings: Outfit (Modern, bold, premium weights).
- Body Content: Inter (Highly readable, clean spacing, optimized hierarchy).

### WEBSITE STRUCTURE & SITEMAP

Design the entire experience on a single, high-performance responsive page (or seamless client-side tabs) divided into the following 12 critical sections:

#### 1. Sticky Navigation & Announcement Bar
- Dynamic announcement bar at the top: Bold Crimson Badge reading "100% OFFLINE ONLY" next to a message stating: "Classes, enrollments, and consultations are conducted exclusively in-person at our physical campus." Provide a direct tap-to-call phone number.
- Sticky Header: Glassmorphism effect (backdrop blur). Left side has a custom logo block with Korean characters (e.g., "서울" - Seoul) and title. Right side has smooth desktop links to: About, Offline Courses, Why Us, EPS-TOPIK, Success Stories, Gallery, FAQ, and Contact.
- CTA Button in Header: Highlighted WhatsApp green button showing a WhatsApp icon and "Chat Now".
- Mobile: Hamburger menu opening a smooth slide-in side drawer featuring the "100% Offline Only" warning banner.

#### 2. Hero Section (First Impression)
- Split Layout (Desktop):
  - Left Side: Clear offline-only indicator badge ("Physical Classroom Classes Only"). Bold, high-converting headline: "Learn Korean Through Professional Offline Training". Subheadline: "Prepare for EPS-TOPIK, TOPIK, or career-oriented Korean language goals with certified physical classroom coaching, strict discipline, and face-to-face expert supervision." 
  - Primary CTAs: A pulsing green button "Chat on WhatsApp" (representing the main conversion target) and a clean outlined button "Visit Our Institute" (scrolls to Map/Address).
  - Right Side: Professional classroom imagery showcasing active, engaged adult students studying face-to-face in a bright, modern language laboratory. Add a floating badge indicating "100% In-Person Interaction".

#### 3. Trust-Building Stats
- Clean, dark slate bar with 4 major statistics cards:
  - "98%" - EPS-TOPIK Pass Rate (intensive physical mock evaluations)
  - "12+" - Certified Offline Trainers (experienced educators for direct guidance)
  - "18 Max" - Students Per Batch (small size limits for individual counseling)
  - "1,850+" - Successful Workers in Korea (visa clearances and employment passes)

#### 4. About the Institute
- Split Grid: Left side has a custom styled frame containing a student workshop image (experiencing Korean culture in class). Right side explains our core physical learning philosophy:
  - "No Online Shortcuts": Emphasize that language cannot be learned through cold screens or recorded webinars; authentic fluency requires physical interaction.
  - "Direct Career Pathing": Explain in-person support for visa documentation, official HRD hiring procedures, and test requirements.
  - "Daily Personal Counseling": Outline that teachers are physically available in the study library during office hours to solve individual doubts.

#### 5. Offline Courses Grid (Tabbed Matrix)
- Category filters to toggle classes dynamically: "All Programs", "General Language", "Exam Preparation", "Career/Job Prep".
- Create beautiful card blocks for:
  1. Beginner Korean Language (Hangul, phonetics, survival conversations)
  2. Intermediate Korean (Grammar, social dialogues, presentations)
  3. EPS-TOPIK Preparation Bootcamp (60-chapter official textbook review, daily CBT lab evaluations)
  4. TOPIK I & II Exam Prep (High-frequency vocabulary, face-to-face writing review)
  5. Korea Visa & Interview Prep (Crash course, Korean body language, mock interviews)
  6. Advanced Korean Mastery (Native slangs, professional honorifics, news readings)
- Each card must display a bright warning badge: "In-Person Class Only" and include duration, batch limits, and 2 actions: "View Syllabus" (Modal overlay) and "Inquire via WA".

#### 6. Interactive Syllabus Details Modal
- When "View Syllabus" is clicked, trigger a smooth overlay populating:
  - Program timings, batch configurations, and exact physical syllabus breakdown.
  - Highlighted conversion box: A customizable text-area containing a prepended WhatsApp message based on the course selected, with a prominent WhatsApp Button. Clicking it automatically opens a URL-encoded WhatsApp link pre-filled with the student's selected query.

#### 7. Why Choose Our Institute (The Offline Advantage)
- A highly readable grid of 6 cards explaining why in-person coaching is superior:
  - Direct Pronunciation Adjustment: Real-time accent corrections that apps/videos cannot provide.
  - Zero Digital Distractions: Entrance to a study hall designed only for focus (textbooks, pen, paper).
  - Peer-to-Peer Speaking Drills: Breaking conversational fear through team exercises in the room.
  - Simulated Examination Hall: Practice in our physical computer lab under strict test conditions.
  - Rigorous Discipline: Mandatory attendance checks, homework inspections, and board dictations.
  - Direct Teacher Office Hours: Face-to-face counseling next to instructors in the library.

#### 8. EPS-TOPIK & TOPIK Lab Hub
- Focus on how the institute prepares students:
  - CBT Practice Lab: A dedicated computer room loaded with official test simulation software.
  - Saturday Mock Assessments: Converting the physical campus into a strict timed test center.
  - Display an interactive "Performance Board" showing: "Success Rate: 98.2%", "Average Score: 188/200", "Workstations: 35".

#### 9. Physical Emigration Roadmap
- Visual horizontal timeline tracking the offline student's journey:
  - Step 1: In-Person Counseling (Walk in, review worksheets, plan routes)
  - Step 2: Offline Class Mastery (Daily 2-hour high-intensity lectures)
  - Step 3: CBT Exam Drills (Physical laboratory mock evaluations)
  - Step 4: Visa & Job Selection (hiring mock interviews, document processing)

#### 10. Student Success Stories
- Interactive fade-in slider carousel displaying physical students who achieved top scores or work visas in Korea.
- Include names, visa categories (e.g., Gyeonggi-do heavy manufacturing, Incheon Selection), and official score certifications.

#### 11. Facility Gallery
- Filterable grid: "Show All", "Classrooms", "CBT Labs", "Culture Workshops".
- Showcases the high-end physical academy space, library setups, Hangeul writing circles, and graduation celebrations.

#### 12. Certified Teachers / Native Instructors
- Profile cards displaying senior bilingually certified professors and native guest speakers.
- Highlight their educational degrees, years of teaching, and specific campus counseling desk hours.

#### 13. FAQ (Addressing Offline Queries)
- Collapsible interactive accordion resolving typical questions:
  - "Do you offer online classes?" (No, we are 100% offline to enforce real quality)
  - "How can I register?" (Visit our campus physically. We support zero online checkout transactions to ensure quality counseling first)
  - "What is the capacity limit?" (We strictly cap cohorts at 15 to 18 students per batch)
  - "What are school hours?" (Admin desk open Mon-Sat, 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM)

#### 14. Conversion CTA Banner
- A high-energy dark navy banner featuring a traditional red Korean seal stamp.
- Headline: "Ready to Take the Next Step Toward Your Future in Korea?"
- Subtitle: "Don't waste time on digital apps. Get professional counseling, study in high-energy offline classrooms, and clear your examinations with confidence."
- Prominent green button: "Chat on WhatsApp Now" and outlined "Get Campus Directions".

#### 15. Contact & Simulated Interactive Map
- Detailed street address, nearby physical landmarks, office timing schedules, and support contact phone.
- A simulated interactive maps widget: An beautifully styled abstract canvas representing streets, landmark blocks ("Central Bank", "Ground Station"), and a pulsing marker pin indicating our building. Include parking and transit access indicators (bus, parking slots).

#### 16. Footer & Sticky Mobile Contact
- Comprehensive dark footer showing address, opening hours, social links, sitemap links, and a strict legal disclaimer: "Seoul Academy supports in-person learning only. No digital accounts or online profiles are registered."
- Mobile Sticky Footer (Bottom Bar): Permanent fixed bar on mobile browsers showing:
  - Left Button: "Call Direct" (opens mobile telephone dialer)
  - Right Button: "WhatsApp Chat" (opens WhatsApp with a custom greeting query)
- Floating Widget: Sticky green circular WhatsApp button with a pulsing ring animation, showing a tooltip "Physical Classroom Inquiry" on hover.

### CORE LOGICAL CODES & DYNAMIC HOOKS

Ensure that:
1. Every WhatsApp CTA has a context-specific pre-populated string that includes terms like "offline classes", "visit your campus", "in-person batch timings", to filter high-intent students immediately.
2. Clicking tab buttons filters courses and gallery items smoothly using entrance fades rather than hard breaks.
3. Mobile drawers and modals use hidden overflow on document body to prevent secondary scrolling.
```
